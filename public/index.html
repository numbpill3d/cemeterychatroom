about the keeper

i am lusynth. 23. scorpio rising. i built this place in late 2003 on an old dell after my parents went to sleep. the other chatrooms were too bright and too loud and full of people pretending to be happy.

this is not a profile shrine anymore. it is a social crypt for night owls, insomniacs, baby bats, and anyone who feels more alive after midnight. we talk in the main chat, we rot together in the forums, we share music that hurts good.

there are no algorithms here. no logging. no clout. just the same five to ten souls circling the same digital graveyard for twenty years. if you found us, you were meant to.

rules are simple: be kind to the other monsters, do not bring the daylight in, and always haunt responsibly.

stay cold,
lusynth
